1
Top handle — drag to set exact start. Snaps to the slot grid but can be released between snaps for a minute-precise time (e.g. 9:50 AM).
2
Partial cell at the bottom. The block ends at 11:45 — a dashed line cuts the 11:30–12:00 slot exactly in half, and the bottom handle sits on it.
3
Handles only appear in Edit mode after a drag selection. Tapping a handle opens a time picker — a mobile-friendly fallback.
Recommended: 30-min slots for most events; switch to 15-min when the organizer sets a non-standard window (e.g. 9:45 AM start). Handles give sub-slot precision without changing the displayed granularity.

How each entry point behaves
1
Attendance row on each stop card — always visible, shows the colored full/partial/absent count. Clicking opens the overview pre-scrolled to that stop. Fastest way to check one stop mid-planning.
2
Conflict badge on the card header — only appears at 2+ conflicts. The sienna color draws the eye; clicking jumps to that stop's conflict callout with the suggested fix.
3
"View overview" in the summary bar — always at the bottom of the panel. Opens the full matrix for all stops at once — for reviewing the whole picture before finalizing.
4
Dedicated tab — the most permanent home, sitting next to Availability, Location, and Details. Accessible from anywhere, not just inside the itinerary.
